A separate quality gate
Review first. Approve next. Export last.
This screen explains the process. No review, approval or export is performed here.


Independent reviewer
Check the exact draft
  • A different, authorized account reviews the content.
  • Check each claim against its source, rights and title promise.
  • Recheck current sources and resolve or disclose contradictions.
  • The review is bound to this version and expires after 24 hours.
Workspace owner / authorized approver
Approve the reviewed version
  • Confirm the version and independent review are current.
  • Edits, changed evidence or revoked reviewer access block old approval.
  • Download an approved team brief or structured content package.
  • Export never publishes or schedules a social post.


Sample status: not reviewed, not approved. There is no approval shortcut in this walkthrough. The production app checks permissions and evidence on the server.
